Where Our Name Comes From

The name thryve youth comes from our commited dedication to unlock the full potential of every young person. We chose this name because we want youth to do more than just get by, we want them to thrive. Even when mental health struggles feel heavy, compressing and pushing you down, we are here to ensure you feel loved, protected, and fully supported. "We want you to thrive" is not just our motto; it is the heartbeat of everything we do.
