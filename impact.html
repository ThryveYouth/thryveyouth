Our Mission

Our mission is to break the stigma surrounding teen mental health by providing safe environments for open conversation, launching vital awareness campaigns, and empowering youth to speak their truth without fear.

Open Source

We believe mental health support should have no barriers. All of our peer mentoship guides, awareness materials, and volunteer-driven movement are completely open-source. It is all free for any community to use, adapt, and share.

Funding What Matters

We rely on donations to fund everything from teen care packages to mental health awareness materials, ensuring our resources remain 100% free and direct towards youth.
