What is Thryve Youth?

Thryve Youth is a non-profit organization founded in Austin, Texas dedicated to supporting the youth of our generation who struggle with mental health challenges. We reduce the gap between the vulnerable young people and the resources they need by providing safe spaces, peer mentorship, and accessible mental health education. Our mission is to empower the next generation from struggling to truly thriving.

Why It Matters

Providing teens with mental health resources is vital as half of all mental health conditions begin by the age of 14, and early support prevents lifelong struggles. Neglecting these psychological conditions pushes a teenager further behind both emotionally and socially.
